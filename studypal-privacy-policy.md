**Privacy Policy — StudyPal by Blaze**

**Last updated: 6 September 2026**

Blaze (“we,” “us,” “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with the StudyPal by Blaze mobile application (“the App”).

1. Information We Collect
Local data only: StudyPal is designed to work fully offline. Your practice history, quiz scores, streak progress, and subject preferences are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us or third parties.

Account information: The current version of the App does not require account creation. If a future version introduces sign‑up/login, we may collect your email address or phone number to manage your account and subscription.

Usage/analytics data: The App does not currently use third‑party analytics or crash‑reporting tools. If we add such services later (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics), we will update this Policy to describe what is collected.

Purchase information: Premium subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details; Google handles this directly under its own privacy policy.

Notifications: The App may send local reminder notifications (e.g., streak reminders). These are generated entirely on your device and do not involve transmitting your data.

2. How We Use Information
Any information collected is used only to:

Provide and maintain the App’s features (practice tracking, streaks, premium access)

Process subscription payments (via Google Play Billing)

Improve the App based on aggregated, anonymized usage patterns (if analytics are added)

Respond to support requests you send us directly

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Third‑Party Content Sources
Past exam questions are sourced from third‑party providers, including ALOC and SdashAPI. These providers may have their own data practices for the content they supply. This Privacy Policy covers only StudyPal by Blaze’s handling of your information as a user of the App.

4. Children’s Privacy
StudyPal is designed for secondary school students preparing for JAMB, WAEC, and NECO, many of whom may be minors.

We do not knowingly collect more personal information from children than is necessary for the App’s core functions.

Parents/guardians may contact us at blazeworldd@outlook.com to review or request deletion of any information.

We comply with Nigeria’s Data Protection Act and Google Play’s Families policies. If future versions introduce accounts or online features, we will implement additional safeguards.

5. Data Storage and Security
Your practice data is stored locally on your device using standard Android storage mechanisms. If cloud sync or backup is introduced later, we will disclose where that data is hosted and how it is secured. We take reasonable measures to protect any information we do collect, but no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

6. Your Choices
You can delete all locally stored App data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its data via Android Settings.

If accounts are introduced in future versions, you will be able to request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at blazeworldd@outlook.com.

7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
